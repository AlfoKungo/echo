Opening Echo…

A little practice, your way.

Vocabulary. Recall the word, then tap to reveal its translation. Tap the left half if you didn’t know it, or the right half if you did. Space or Enter reveals; the arrow keys grade. Undo takes back your latest answer.

Two successful recalls after your latest miss finish a practice cycle; a miss resets the count to zero and moves the card to the failed stack. FSRS schedules the next review. Each direction has its own memory. Category, daily allowance and pronunciation speed live in Vocabulary options.

Your working deck has three stacks: unseen, last answered incorrectly, and last answered correctly. Unseen counts the first stack plus new words available within your daily allowance. Seen counts the other two stacks together. Finished cards count in neither. Undo restores the card, its stack and the counts.

The first ten unseen cards come first. After that, practice mixes three unseen, two failed and one successful card, skipping empty stacks. Every card enters or re-enters a stack at a random position. The daily allowance renews at 04:00 on your Mac; unfinished cards keep their stacks and progress.

Extra practice. In Vocabulary options, or when you reach the end, add +5, +10, +20 or any number of new words for today only; your daily allowance is unchanged and the extra ends at 04:00. You can also review finished words early — 10, 20 or any number of the ones due soonest, or the ones you missed most. Asking for more than are available takes all of them. An early review is a real review: finishing it updates the word’s schedule, with a smaller step because it came early.

Your words (the search button in Vocabulary) lists every word. Choose Studied to see the words you have practised, most recent first, with when you last studied each, how often you missed it and its next review. Hide translations lets you test yourself and reveal each answer; checking the list never changes your progress.

Grammar & sentences. Seven units take you from A1 towards B1. Open a topic to read its rule, then practise a round of 10 questions: build, rewrite, complete, repair, choose, translate and conjugate. Check your answer, try again with specific feedback, or ask for a hint or the rule; each round records what you did on your own, with help, or need to revisit. Ctrl/⌘ + Enter checks or continues.

Tests. A topic test asks 15 questions across the whole topic, and each unit ends with a test of 20 questions across its topics. Tests have no hints, rule or shown answers, and each question takes one try; you see the right answer and why straight after. Your best and latest scores are kept, and anything you miss in a test comes back first the next time you practise that topic.

Audio is your choice. It starts off. Turn Audio on to hear the French recording each time you explicitly reveal a vocabulary card, in either direction. Turning it on does not play anything; turning it off stops playback. Browsing pronunciation buttons play just that word.

Listen mode. In French → English, turn on Listen under the card to hear each word instead of reading it. The word plays when the card appears; tap the speaker to hear it again, then tap the card to see the French and its meaning and answer as usual. It uses the same progress as reading.

Your vocabulary progress, grammar rounds and test scores live on your Mac, shared by your phone and desktop. Your direction, category and audio mode are remembered on this device. Echo always opens on Home, and Back returns to the previous screen.
