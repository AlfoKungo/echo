THE ECHO METHOD

A word becomes
second nature.

Recall first. Reveal the answer. Then be honest about whether you knew it. This is vocabulary practice, not a course.

  1. A small daily rhythm.Start with 30 new words a day in each direction. French → English and English → French have separate memories. Your daily allowance is shared across category filters, resets at 04:00 on your Mac’s clock, and does not accumulate on days you miss. Change the goal on the home screen; due reviews are not capped by it.
  2. Know it twice.Each due cycle needs two consecutive “Knew it” answers, with other cards between recalls when available. “Didn’t know” resets that word’s count to zero. Tap to reveal, then tap the left half if you didn’t know it or the right half if you did.
  3. Return at the right time.FSRS schedules the next long-term review once you complete a cycle. Echo’s two-recall cycle deliberately differs from Anki’s exact learning-step timing.

Your progress lives on your Mac, shared by your phone and desktop. French pronunciation uses Chatterbox recordings. Nothing plays automatically.
